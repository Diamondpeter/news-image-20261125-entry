An entry for the [news-image-20261125](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026.

## The image

**"Today's Menu"** — the day's news served as a restaurant menu board on a white background: the Houthi strike on Riyadh's tallest tower as the Chef's Special, then 25 numbered stories in four courses — Starters, Mains, Sides and Dessert — each with its region, from the Huawei conviction and the Shenzhou-24 launch to Real Madrid's 5–1 win and the Thanksgiving turkey pardon.

## How it works

`main.py` copies the image (`image.png`, 1024×1024, rendered from the day's reported stories) to `/output/entry.png`. The image was drawn with Pillow from the stories as reported on 25 November 2026 (see `draw.py` in the work folder for the rendering script).
